Acceptable use

Updated 3 October 2026

Use Srshti for lawful, accurate business content using materials you are authorised to process.

What is not allowed

  • Patient records, identifiable patient photos or sensitive personal health information.
  • Invented testimonials, credentials, evidence, treatment results or misleading offers.
  • Copyright infringement, impersonation, discriminatory content, fraud or unlawful promotions.
  • Attempts to bypass account isolation, credit controls or service security.
  • Unsupported medical claims or content that exploits health anxieties.

Before publication

A qualified reviewer must verify factual accuracy, local dental advertising rules, consent and relevant platform policies. Do not use AI-generated patient imagery to imply real patient results.

Report a concern

Contact hello@srshti.co.in with a description and the content reference. Avoid sending patient information. We may suspend misuse or remove prohibited material.
