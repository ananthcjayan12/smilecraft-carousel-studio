A considered approach to dental marketing.

Updated 3 October 2026

Srshti supports responsible review with editable drafts, source references and approval steps. This guide is a starting point for clinic teams in Australia, the United States, Ireland and the United Kingdom; it is not a comprehensive legal checklist or certification.

Australia

Review advertising against current Ahpra guidance for regulated health services. Pay particular attention to misleading claims, clinical testimonials, unreasonable expectations and promotions that encourage unnecessary treatment. Check whether additional requirements apply to the services or products promoted.

Ahpra advertising hub ↗

Ireland

Review public communications against the Dental Council’s current professional guidance. Check accuracy, professional titles, credentials and the way services are described, along with applicable privacy and consumer obligations.

Dental Council of Ireland ↗

United States

Advertising must be truthful, and claims need an appropriate basis. Also check your state dental board’s rules and any applicable patient privacy and consent requirements. Srshti is not a patient-data system and does not represent that using it makes a clinic HIPAA compliant.

FTC truth-in-advertising guidance ↗

United Kingdom

Your practice should check the General Dental Council’s guidance on advertising and current ASA/CAP requirements before sharing dental content. Check factual claims, professional descriptions, privacy and consent with a qualified reviewer.
