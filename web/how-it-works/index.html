CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES

Srshti prepares. Your team decides.

Srshti

  • Plans topics from reviewed patient-information sources
  • Writes slide copy and captions
  • Designs artwork with your logo and style
  • Flags guarantees, invented numbers and unsupported claims

Your team

  • Confirms clinic details and brand
  • Reviews and approves every piece
  • Downloads and publishes from your own accounts
  • Remains responsible for local advertising rules

Srshti does not connect to social accounts, schedule or publish posts. Generation starts when you request it. Never upload patient records, identifiable patient photos or sensitive health information.
