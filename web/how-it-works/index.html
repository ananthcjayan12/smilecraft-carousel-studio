How Srshti works

Updated 3 October 2026

From your practice’s brand to downloadable content, in three steps.

1. Confirm your practice and brand

Add authorised business details, your logo and colours. Confirm the information and select a visual style. Do not upload patient records, identifiable photographs or sensitive health information.

2. Choose a workflow

Request a planned week of two carousels, two posts and two stories. Use the run-from-start-to-finish option to prepare content and artwork, or work through the stages yourself. Alternatively, create individual pieces from your own brief. Both options share your monthly generation credits.

Weekly generation starts when you request it. It is not an unattended calendar scheduler.

3. Review and download

Check facts, sources, copy and artwork. Your practice remains responsible for final accuracy and the requirements in your market. Download approved images and captions. Srshti does not connect to social accounts, schedule or publish posts.
