Payments, renewal & refunds

Updated 3 October 2026

Before you pay

All listed plans are in USD. Applicable taxes and any third-party conversion fees are additional. We confirm your plan, total charge, payment method, allocation period and access dates before requesting payment. Checkout and activation are handled manually; signing in does not charge you.

Usage credits

Credits cover writing and artwork generation, including revisions. Artwork generation currently uses 10 credits per image; writing uses 1 or 2 credits depending on the request. A five-image carousel therefore uses at least 50 artwork credits plus writing. Your balance and the current generation action determine availability. A credit allowance does not promise a fixed content volume.

Your credits are allocated for each confirmed paid month. Confirm the allocation and any expiry details with the team before purchase. Do not assume unused credits roll over or that a top-up is included.

Renewal and stopping

No automatic card renewal runs at launch. You can choose not to purchase the next month or ask support to stop future activation. Existing content and access after the paid period depend on the agreed account arrangements; download needed content beforehand.

Refund requests

If activation has not occurred and no paid credits have been used, request a refund of that payment. For duplicate payments, technical failures or unused paid access that cannot be delivered, contact support for investigation and an appropriate refund or credit adjustment. Consumed generation is generally not refundable solely because you dislike the result, subject to applicable statutory rights.

Email ananth.c.jayan@gmail.com with the account email, invoice reference and issue. We will confirm the outcome and processing details by email. Nothing here limits mandatory consumer rights applicable in your jurisdiction.
