Cookies & local storage

Updated 3 October 2026

Srshti uses essential session cookies for Google sign-in and workspace security. The app also remembers workspace choices such as the selected clinic in browser storage.

Optional tracking

These public pages currently load no advertising pixel or optional analytics scripts. There is no optional tracking to accept or reject. A consent control must be introduced before adding non-essential tracking where consent is required.

Your controls

Your browser lets you remove site cookies and storage. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in. You can clear the remembered clinic below without deleting your workspace data.
