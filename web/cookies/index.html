Cookies & local storage

Updated 3 October 2026

Srshti uses essential session cookies for Google sign-in and workspace security. The app also remembers workspace choices such as the selected clinic in browser storage.

Optional tracking

When configured, the optional OpenAI Ads pixel measures visits and confirmed appointments only after you allow advertising measurement. Its first-party attribution cookies are __oppref (up to 30 days) and __obref (up to 365 days). Browser restrictions may shorten these periods. No pixel runs when it is not configured or you reject measurement. The Calendly booking calendar on the demo page loads when you open that page and has its own privacy and cookie controls.

Your controls

Your browser lets you remove site cookies and storage. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in. You can clear the remembered clinic below without deleting your workspace data.
