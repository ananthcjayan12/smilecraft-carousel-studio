About Srshti

Updated 3 October 2026

Srshti is an independent content studio for dental clinic teams. It helps you plan topics, draft copy, create branded artwork, review and download social content.

Designed around your team

Our $80 USD monthly founding plan for the first 100 paid clinics includes 1,000 credits for planned or individual content. Our focus is a clear workflow for clinics in Australia, the United States, Ireland and the United Kingdom. Your team confirms clinic information, checks sources and approves content. Srshti does not provide clinical advice or publish on your behalf at launch.
