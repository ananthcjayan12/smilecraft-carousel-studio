THE DETAILS, ANSWERED

Before you open your studio.

What does Srshti create?+

Branded carousels, single posts, stories and captions. Review content and download artwork for your team to publish. The animated example illustrates a possible mix, not a guaranteed plan output.

Does Srshti guarantee dental marketing compliance?+

No. It supports review through editable copy, source references and approval steps. A qualified clinic reviewer must check local advertising rules, claims, consent and privacy before publishing.

Will it post automatically?+

No. At launch, download your content pack and publish through your own accounts or your social media team.

Is pricing in my local currency?+

All prices are in USD. Australian and Irish customers also pay in USD. Applicable taxes and any bank conversion fees are additional.

Do I need to pay before setting up?+

You can sign in and begin clinic setup. Generation requires team activation after payment is confirmed. Signing in does not start a paid subscription.

How do credits work?+

Writing and artwork use credits, including revisions. Your allocation is added for each paid month. Ask the team to confirm your balance, allocation period and renewal before purchase.

What should I upload?+

Only authorised business information and brand assets. Do not upload patient records, identifiable patient images or sensitive health information.
