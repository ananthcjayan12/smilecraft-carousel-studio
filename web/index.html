COMPLIANCE-AWARE DESIGN

Guardrails built in, not bolted on.

Source checks, editable drafts and your team’s final approval. Content creation and downloads; your team publishes.

◎

Approved facts only

Copy must trace back to reviewed patient-information sources or the clinic facts you confirmed. Wording outside them is sent back.

⊘

Outcome claims blocked

“Guaranteed”, “painless”, “100%”, “risk-free”, “instant results” and “best clinic” are rejected before artwork is made.

guaranteedpainless100%risk-free
#

No invented numbers

Any statistic that isn’t in an approved fact is rejected, so figures can’t slip in.

✦

Your real logo, nothing invented

Artwork uses your supplied logo and confirmed details. Check the generated images for accuracy before sharing.

☰

Education before promotion

Informational slides carry no sales language. A restrained booking invitation appears only on the final slide.

⛨

No patient data needed

Srshti is a content tool, not a patient system. You never need to upload records or patient photos.

Credit usage and payment details +

Each generated image uses 10 credits. Brief preparation and validation use 1 credit per request; writing uses 2. A standard six-piece week uses about 164 credits: 140 for 14 images and 24 for preparing, writing and checking six pieces. Five weekly packs use about 820 credits, leaving 180 for brand-style setup or revisions.

Extra artwork, rewrites and style generations use the same balance. Your final volume depends on the mix and revisions you choose; credits are not unlimited. The app plans a week when you request it, rather than running on a calendar schedule. Your team reviews, downloads and publishes the content; Srshti does not schedule or publish posts.

THE DETAILS, ANSWERED

Before you open your studio.

What does Srshti create?+

Branded carousels, single posts, stories and captions. Review content and download artwork for your team to publish. The animated example illustrates a possible mix, not a guaranteed plan output.

How does it compare with hiring a designer?+

A designer brings bespoke creative judgement; Srshti brings speed, consistency and a predictable monthly price for routine educational content. Many clinics use Srshti for the weekly rhythm and keep a designer for campaigns. The calculator above shows an estimate for your mix.

Which compliance standards does Srshti follow?+

Copy is limited to approved source facts and confirmed clinic details, and outcome claims, invented numbers, testimonials and prices are rejected. These guardrails are designed around guidance such as Ahpra’s advertising guidelines, the Dental Council of Ireland’s code, the FTC Act and the ADA Code. They support your review but are not a certification.

Does Srshti guarantee dental marketing compliance?+

No. It supports review through editable copy, source references and approval steps. A qualified clinic reviewer must check local advertising rules, claims, consent and privacy before publishing.

Will it post automatically?+

No. At launch, download your content pack and publish through your own accounts or your social media team.

Is pricing in my local currency?+

All prices are in USD. Customers in Australia, the United States, Ireland and the United Kingdom pay in USD. Applicable taxes and any bank conversion fees are additional.

Do I need to pay before setting up?+

You can sign in and begin clinic setup. Generation requires team activation after payment is confirmed. Signing in does not start a paid subscription.

How do credits work?+

Writing and artwork use credits, including revisions. Your allocation is added for each paid month. Ask the team to confirm your balance, allocation period and renewal before purchase.

What should I upload?+

Only authorised business information and brand assets. Do not upload patient records, identifiable patient images or sensitive health information.
